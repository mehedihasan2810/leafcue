# LeafCue — App Store Positioning

## One-line description

LeafCue is a private offline plant care tracker for watering, fertilizing,
reminders, photos, journals, growth logs, and plant health notes. No account
required.

## Subtitle (iOS) / Short description (Android)

Private offline plant care tracker.

## Long description

LeafCue helps you keep your plants alive and thriving without giving up your
data. Add your plants, set watering and fertilizing schedules, get gentle
local reminders, and journal what you observe. Everything stays on your
device — no accounts, no servers, no analytics.

### What you get

- Care schedules for watering, fertilizing, misting, pruning, repotting and
  more, with smart defaults you can override per plant.
- A daily Today view that shows what needs care now, with one-tap complete
  and snooze.
- A simple weekly Calendar to plan ahead.
- Per-plant detail with photos, journal entries, growth measurements, and
  health observations.
- Local notifications scheduled by your phone, with quiet hours and a
  discreet preview style for the lock screen.
- A built-in plant preset library to seed common species with sensible
  defaults.
- Rooms and shelves so you can browse by where your plants live.

### Privacy

- Local-first by design. All plant data lives on your device in SQLite.
- No accounts. No sign-in. No identifiers collected.
- No network calls for plant data, no analytics, no telemetry.
- Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS / Android and never leave the
  device.
- Backups are JSON files you control. They go where you tell them — Files,
  AirDrop, email, your favourite cloud — never to LeafCue.

### Backup and restore

LeafCue can export a versioned JSON backup of your plants, schedules, logs,
and settings. Re-import it later with two modes:

- Merge — append plant data with fresh IDs, keep your current settings.
- Replace — wipe local data and restore everything from the backup.

Photo files are not bundled in the JSON yet (planned for a future update).
Keep your photo library safe via your device's normal photo backup.
